Welcome

Welcome to the official website of Lozells Central Mosque - A Vision brought to Life. Lozells Central Mosque is a state of the arts, purpose-built mosque and youth space located in Bimingham. We serve as a welcoming space of worship, education, spiritual growth and service to all in the local and wider community.

You can access this website for all of your needs, whenever you need, wherever you need it. Feel free to explore this website to learn more about our community inspired institution.
